## Examination overview and credential details

NCA-GENM is the NVIDIA-Certified Associate Generative AI Multimodal credential. It is intended for developers contributing to datasets, model training, testing, debugging, and deployment across modalities such as text, images, and audio.

The verified examination details include:

- Associate level.
- A duration of 60 minutes.
- A listed fee of USD 125.
- English language delivery.
- Online remote proctoring through Certiverse.
- A basic understanding of generative AI as the prerequisite.
- Two-year credential validity, with examination retake available for recertification.

The official introduction says 50 questions, while the details table lists 50–60 multiple-choice questions. Confirm the count during registration rather than relying on one statement as settled. Passing candidates receive a digital badge and an optional certificate.

## Cover the complete seven-domain scope

Core Machine Learning and AI Knowledge represents 20%. It includes training stability, loss functions, feature engineering, model comparison, cross-validation, residual connections, statistical evaluation, transfer learning, prompting, and frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.

Data Analysis contributes 10%, covering insight extraction, attention-map material, visualization, relationships, and factors influencing results.

Experimentation carries 25%. Review model development and testing, preparation of data from different sources, explainability, data consistency, accuracy, and effectiveness.

Multimodal Data represents 15%. It includes supervised deployment and evaluation, RAG and related LLM use cases, Python tooling, required components, monitoring, and supervised scripts.

Performance Optimization contributes 10% through efficiency, accuracy, hyperparameters, transfer learning, and supervised training improvement.

Software Development accounts for 15%, including collaboration, quality, prompting, U-Net, and CLIP-related learning. Trustworthy AI completes the outline at 5%, covering ethics, privacy, consent, technologies, and bias reduction.
